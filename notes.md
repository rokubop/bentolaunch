## What's new

- **Renamed to BentoLaunch.** `bentopick.toml` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\bentopick`
  rename themselves on first run. Settings and paired browsers survive.
- **Update both halves.** The pairing proof carries the name, so app and
  extension changed together. Load the new `bentolaunch-extension.zip`, pair
  again. A stale extension is told to update, not refused in silence.
- **All apps** - last square in Apps. Everything installed, Store apps included.
  The box only mirrors your taskbar, so unpinned apps had no way onto the panel.
- **All bookmarks** - last square in Bookmarks. Whole tree, not just the bar.
  Flat, with each folder on line two. Needs a paired browser. Read-only.
- **The extension keeps its ID.** Moving its folder no longer unpairs browsers.
- **Defaults fit on screen.** Launch tops the left lane, Bookmarks the right,
  Active and Browsing under them. Bookmarks capped at 10; 32 ran off the bottom.
- **Logo is the fourth square of the grid** - same cell, same size. Colour is
  the only difference.
